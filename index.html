How TRIÉA started

IT STARTED
WITH AN IDEA.

After losing one earring from a pair I loved one too many times, I remember waking up one morning, looking at my husband and saying:

“Why don’t I just create my own?”

The more I thought about it, the more I realized how many women I knew had experienced the exact same thing — a jewelry box filled with single earrings from pairs they once loved.

That’s when a small frustration started to feel like an idea worth pursuing.

TRIÉA was created to do things a little differently: three earrings instead of two — your pair, plus your spare.

— Alina, Founder
